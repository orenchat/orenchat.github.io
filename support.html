Common questions

How do I sign in?
Tap “Continue with Apple”. One tap, no passwords, and you can keep your email hidden.

Why hasn’t Oren replied?
Oren is a real person and can’t answer while busy or sleeping, but always tries to reply. After your very first message you’ll get a short welcome Oren wrote in advance, marked “Automatic reply”, so you know it arrived; every reply after that is Oren personally. Turn on notifications in your profile to hear back right away.

How do I change my name, photo or caption?
Tap the gear at the top of the chat.

How do I delete my account?
Tap the gear, then “Delete my account”. Your messages, photos and profile are deleted for good.
