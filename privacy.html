Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

The short version: Oren keeps only what the chat needs: your sign-in, your profile and your messages with Oren. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data. You can delete everything from the app at any time.

What we collect

  • Sign in with Apple: an anonymous Apple identifier and the email Apple shares (which can be a private relay address if you choose “Hide My Email”). If you allow it, Apple also shares your first name.
  • Your profile: the name, photo and caption you add. Oren can see them next to your messages.
  • Your messages and photos with Oren, and whether they’ve been seen.
  • A push notification token for your iPhone, so we can tell you when Oren replies. You can turn notifications off in the app.

Photos are resized before upload, and location information stored in photos is removed.

How we use it

Only to run the chat: to sign you in, show your messages to you and Oren, and send notifications. We don’t use analytics or advertising, we don’t track you across apps or websites, and we don’t sell or share your data with anyone.

Who can see your messages

Only you and Oren. Your chat isn’t visible to other users.

Where it’s stored

Your data is stored securely with our hosting provider, Supabase, in the United States. Notifications are delivered through Apple’s push notification service.

Deleting your data

In the app, tap the gear, then “Delete my account”. Your profile, messages and photos are deleted for good, and your Sign in with Apple link to Oren is removed. Oren may also delete a chat at any time.

Children

Oren isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above.

Questions about privacy? Please email us.
